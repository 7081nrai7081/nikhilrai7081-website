What It Is

A community, not a company.

VYNTRA is an independent community initiative by Nikhil Rai, built around one idea: Lucknow deserves more ways to meet people and spend weekends well. It's free, informal, and organised through a WhatsApp group rather than an app or a subscription.

Questions

Good to know.

What is VYNTRA?

A free social discovery community based in Lucknow, India, for music nights, open mics, creative workshops and social meetups.

Who created VYNTRA?

It's an independent community initiative by Nikhil Rai, built to give Lucknow more ways to meet people and spend weekends well.

Is it free to join?

Yes: joining the WhatsApp community is completely free, with no obligation, and you can leave at any time.

Where can I find the latest events?

Inside the WhatsApp community, or at vyntra.club/community.

Want in?
