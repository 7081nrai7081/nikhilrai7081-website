About bigha and biswa in UP

Unlike square feet, gaj, acres and hectares — which are fixed, standard units everywhere — bigha and biswa sizes vary by district in Uttar Pradesh. The figures above use the common convention for the Lucknow/Purvanchal region (1 bigha = 20 biswa = 27,225 sq ft, 1 biswa ≈ 1,361 sq ft). Some western UP districts (around Meerut, Muzaffarnagar, Bulandshahr) use a much smaller bigha — under a third of this size. We don't have confident district-level data for Kanpur specifically, so always confirm the local conversion with your tehsil/revenue office or a local surveyor before relying on it for a real transaction.

Fixed conversions used above

  • 1 square yard (gaj) = 9 square feet
  • 1 square metre ≈ 10.7639 square feet
  • 1 acre = 43,560 square feet
  • 1 hectare = 107,639 square feet
